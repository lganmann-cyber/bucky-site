# App icon — art direction for designer swap

The `AppIcon.appiconset` ships empty (placeholder). Drop a single 1024×1024
PNG in and add its filename to `Contents.json`.

## Direction

A rendered **vintage film box**, straight-on, filling ~85% of the canvas.

- **Background:** warm cream `#F2EDE2`, very subtle paper grain, slight
  darkening toward corners (aged cardstock, not a vignette effect).
- **The box:** faded film-box orange `#C75B39` as the dominant panel color,
  with a cream band across the middle third. Edges slightly worn — tiny
  chips of ink loss on the corners, one soft scuff. Print misregistration
  (offset ~1px) on one color pass sells the era.
- **Wordmark:** `OLDSNAP` in condensed uppercase (Oswald-class), near-black
  `#2B2A26`, letterspaced, centered on the cream band. Below it, small
  letterspaced caps: `COLOR · 36 EXP · ISO 200` as dressing.
- **No border radius** on the box art itself (iOS masks the icon square
  anyway). No gloss, no gradients beyond paper aging, no camera glyphs.
- Must read at 60×60: the orange box + dark wordmark is the whole icon.
  If detail fights legibility, drop the detail.

## Don'ts

- No real brand trade dress (Kodak yellow/red arrangement, Fuji green,
  Polaroid rainbow stripe). Ours is orange-on-cream, one accent, flat print.
- No photograph inside the icon, no emoji, no film-strip clichés.
