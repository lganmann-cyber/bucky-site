# 35C — '90s single-use disposable · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** scanned shots from 90s consumer disposables
(FunSaver/QuickSnap class hardware: fixed ~f/10 plastic meniscus lens, 1/100s
shutter, 800-speed consumer color negative, direct onboard flash). Sources:
Flickr disposable-camera groups, lab sample scans of consumer 800 neg, family
archive scans of party/vacation shots, 90s print-film scans posted by film labs.

## Observed characteristics

**Color.** Overall warm — lab prints of consumer neg lean golden, and the
tungsten/flash mix pushes it further. Reds render forward and slightly orange;
greens are muted olive rather than emerald. Skin tones are flattered: warm,
slightly rosy, low detail (flash flattening). Shadows go warm brown, never
blue. Whites near the flash carry a yellow-warm cap.

**Contrast.** High for a negative film — these were printed on punchy consumer
paper. Toe is crushed (interiors fall off to black fast beyond flash range),
shoulder is fast but still film-like: blown foreheads hold a trace of texture
instead of digital-clipping.

**Flash signature (the defining trait).** Direct on-axis flash ~1 m range:
subject hot (often +1 stop on faces), background collapsing to darkness.
Falloff center is rarely dead-center — cameras were tilted. Red-eye common
(not simulated at v1 — would require face detection; noted for v2).

**Grain.** Heavy, obvious, clumpy — 800-speed neg scanned on lab minilab
scanners. Mostly luma but with visible color speckle in shadows. Grain is
strongest in mid-shadows; flash-blown highlights look comparatively clean.

**Lens.** Soft everywhere, softer at edges; visible vignette from the tiny
plastic lens; mild lateral CA on high-contrast edges near corners.

**Artifacts.** Orange quartz-date stamp bottom-right on a large share of
consumer scans ('96 12 24 format, LED bloom). Occasional lab dust. Exposure
scatter shot-to-shot is large — one fixed shutter/aperture for every scene.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.c35`)

| Observation | Parameter |
|---|---|
| Warm golden cast | `temperature 0.30`, warm highlight tint |
| Punchy consumer-print contrast | S-curves w/ crushed toe, `gamma 1.04` |
| Blue suppressed relative to red | `curveB` end 0.96 vs `curveR` 1.0 |
| Film shoulder on flash highlights | `highlightRolloff 0.65` |
| Heavy clumpy 800-speed grain | `intensity 0.22, size 2.2, shadowWeight 0.7` |
| Shadow color speckle | `chromaMix 0.18` |
| Flash hot-center/dark-edge | `flashFalloff 0.65` + wandering center |
| Plastic-lens softness + vignette | `bloom 1.2`, `vignette 0.9` |
| Date stamp culture | `dateStampDefault = true`, retro-random dates |
| One-exposure-fits-nothing scatter | `exposureJitter 0.14` |

## Iteration log

- v1: grain 0.30 read as digital noise on 12 MP sources → reduced to 0.22 and
  raised size to 2.2‰ so clumps survive downscaling to feed-size.
- v1: symmetric flash falloff looked like a vignette preset → offset the
  center per-shot (±6% x, −4…+10% y) and lifted the core +10%.
- v2: pulled saturation 1.15 → 1.08; reference scans are warm but not vivid.
