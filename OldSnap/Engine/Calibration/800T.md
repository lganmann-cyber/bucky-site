# 800T — tungsten cine film at night · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** night-street photography shot on
tungsten-balanced 800-speed cinema film respooled for stills (the look that
dominates film TikTok/IG): lab scan galleries, Lomography/Flickr communities,
motion-picture stock reference charts.

## Observed characteristics

**Halation (the money trait).** The remjet anti-halation backing is removed in
the stills respool, so bright light sources bloom through the base and
reflect back — a red-orange halo hugging every practical light, neon sign,
and specular highlight. It is tight and *colored*: red-orange core fading
fast; it does not gray out like ordinary digital bloom. Threshold is high —
only genuine lights and speculars halate, not bright walls.

**Color.** Tungsten-balanced: under daylight/LED the frame cools toward
blue-teal; under tungsten it's near-neutral warm. Signature grade: teal-cyan
shadows + warm orange highlights around practicals. Blacks are slightly
lifted and cool. Neon reds/oranges glow; greens go slightly cyan.

**Contrast.** Cine negative is low-contrast by stills standards — long
shoulder, soft toe, everything printable. Night shots keep shadow detail
where a phone would crush it.

**Grain.** Visible but refined for 800 speed — tighter than the disposable's
consumer neg, mild chroma component, obvious in midtone walls and sky.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.t800`)

| Observation | Parameter |
|---|---|
| Red-orange halo on lights only | `halation threshold 0.72, tint (1.0, 0.30, 0.10), intensity 0.85` |
| Halo hugs the source | `radius 22‰` (tight relative to bloom) |
| Tungsten balance cools scenes | `temperature −0.28` |
| Teal shadows / warm practicals | shadow tint (−0.02, 0.012, 0.032), highlight tint (0.04, 0.02, −0.01) |
| Lifted cine blacks, long shoulder | `blackLift 0.045`, `highlightRolloff 0.75` |
| Refined 800 grain | `intensity 0.15, size 1.8, chromaMix 0.2` |

## Iteration log

- v1: threshold 0.6 halated white shirts → raised to 0.72 so only lights bloom.
- v1: halation via plain bloom grayed out → tint now multiplies the glow
  *before* screen-compositing so the halo stays saturated red-orange.
- v2: added small blue lift in `curveB` toe so night skies sit blue-green
  rather than black — matches scan references.
