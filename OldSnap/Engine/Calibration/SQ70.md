# SQ-70 — '70s white-frame instant · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** scans of 70s integral instant prints (original
era prints, not modern reissue film): family archives, auction/collector
scans, instant-photography community galleries.

## Observed characteristics

- **Milky blacks.** Dye prints never reach true black — deepest shadow is a
  soft charcoal with a cyan drift. The #1 tell.
- **Cyan-teal shadows, cream highlights.** Dye fade pushes shadows cool and
  highlights toward warm cream; overall saturation is low.
- **Soft focus.** Single-element plastic lens + slight print texture: gentle
  bloom across the frame, no crisp edges anywhere.
- **Compressed dynamic range.** Both ends squeezed; scenes look "developed to
  the middle."
- **The frame.** Even white border, thick chin. Aged prints show faint edge
  shadow where the frame meets the image well.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.sq70`)

- Milky blacks: `blackLift 0.10` + curve toes starting at 0.06–0.08
- Cyan shadow drift: shadow tint (−0.015, 0.012, 0.028)
- Cream highlights: highlight tint (0.03, 0.022, 0.005), `saturation 0.86`
- Softness: `bloom 2.2` (largest in lineup besides P110)
- Frame: `FrameStyle.instantWhite` with 5.5% border / 20% chin + edge stroke

## Iteration log

- v1: blackLift 0.16 looked like a bad "faded" filter → 0.10 with curve-toe
  lift reads as print, not preset.
- v2: grain size set to 0.9‰ (largest in lineup) at low intensity — instant "grain" is really
  dye cloud texture: big, faint, slightly colored (`chromaMix 0.25`).
- v3 (device test): every stock's grain size was ~3x too coarse — 2‰-class
  clumps render ~9 px wide on 12 MP and read as circular blobs, amplified
  by Lanczos-upscaled noise in the CI fallback. All sizes recalibrated to
  0.35–0.95‰ and the fallback now scales noise nearest-neighbor with a
  0.25x clump blur.
