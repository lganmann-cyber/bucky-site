# DC-2000 — early-2000s CCD digicam · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** sample galleries and archived personal photos
from 2000–2005 consumer CCD compacts (2–4 MP class), digicam-revival community
uploads, dpreview-era sample archives.

## Observed characteristics

**Color.** CCD color science is its own thing: saturated but *narrow* — vivid
primaries with a slight magenta bias in mids, skies that go cyan-blue, and
skin that flushes pink under flash. No film warmth; white balance is
frequently slightly wrong (cool indoors, warm at dusk).

**Highlights.** The signature failure: highlights clip *hard*. A window in the
background is a white rectangle. No shoulder, no rolloff — digital death.
This is the single most identifying trait; any softness here reads "film" and
breaks the look.

**Resolution.** 2–3 MP upscaled to modern sizes = soft blocky detail with
crispy edges from aggressive in-camera sharpening. Sharpening halos on
branches/wires against sky are period-correct.

**Fringing.** Purple-magenta fringing on high-contrast edges (blown sky
through leaves), strongest off-center.

**Noise.** Shadow noise is fine-grained and strongly *chromatic* — red/blue
confetti in dark areas, nearly absent in well-lit zones.

**Flash.** Harsh, centered, shorter range than film-flash but similar
hot-face/dark-room geometry.

**Timestamp.** Orange/yellow digital timestamp bottom-right, very common.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.dc2000`)

| Observation | Parameter |
|---|---|
| Vivid, narrow CCD color, magenta bias | `saturation 1.18`, `tint 0.05` |
| Hard digital clip | `highlightRolloff 0.05` |
| Low-res crunch + halos | `resolutionCrush 0.42`, `sharpen 0.7` |
| Purple fringing at edges | `chromaticAberration 0.6`, `purpleFringe 0.8` |
| Chroma-heavy shadow noise | `grain chromaMix 0.7, shadowWeight 0.9` |
| Harsh flash geometry | `flashFalloff 0.45` |
| Timestamp culture | `dateStampDefault = true` |

## Iteration log

- v1: resolutionCrush 0.25 destroyed faces at feed-size; 0.42 keeps the
  "3 MP" read while faces survive. Crush skipped on live preview for fps.
- v1: film-style luma grain read wrong → moved almost all noise into chroma.
- v2: added post-crush sharpen (order matters: crush → sharpen) so halos ring
  the *upscaled* edges the way in-camera processing did.
