# HF-72 — '80s half-frame 35mm · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** half-frame camera scans (Pen-class compacts):
diptych scans from labs that print two half-frames per 35mm frame, community
half-frame galleries.

## Observed characteristics

- **The diptych IS the format.** Labs scan two adjacent half-frames as one
  35mm frame: two portrait images separated by a thin unexposed gutter.
  Users shot pairs deliberately — the pairing is the creative act.
- **Grain up ~40%.** Half the negative area enlarged twice as much: consumer
  neg grain reads bigger and softer than full-frame.
- **Ordinary consumer color otherwise.** Warm, gentle, slightly muted — the
  base stock is everyday 200/400 neg, so color sits near SUN 200 but quieter.
- **Slight softness.** Compact fixed lenses, small format: never crisp.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.hf72`)

- Diptych: `FrameStyle.halfFrameDiptych`; `FrameRenderer.diptych` joins two
  developed halves with a 4.5% near-black warm gutter at 3:2 landscape
- Grain: `intensity 0.16, size 2.0` (vs SUN 200's 0.10/1.4)
- Color: SUN-adjacent warmth, `saturation 0.98`
- Softness: `bloom 1.0`, mild vignette per half

## Iteration log

- v1: each half got its own vignette *after* pairing — wrong; vignette now
  renders per half before composition, like two exposures on one frame.
