# L-79 — '70s plastic toy camera · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** Lomography community galleries and toy-camera
scans (plastic-lens box cameras with notorious light seals).

## Observed characteristics

- **Chaos is the character.** No two frames match: exposure swings ±1 stop,
  color shifts warm/cool, leaks come and go. Consistency would be a bug.
- **Heavy mechanical vignette.** The plastic lens can't cover the frame —
  corners fall off hard and slightly asymmetric.
- **Light leaks.** Warm orange/red streaks entering from frame edges (film
  gate/back seal), varying position and strength per shot.
- **Oversaturation.** Community processing culture (cross-process, punchy
  scans): saturated, red-forward, crushed-ish blue.
- **Plastic-lens glow.** Strong spherical aberration bloom + edge CA.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.l79`)

- Variance: the widest `VarianceParams` in the lineup (`exposureJitter 0.28`)
- Vignette: `strength 1.6, radius 1.25` — strongest in the app
- Leaks: `lightLeakChance 0.6, strength 0.7`; per-seed side/color/breadth
- Saturation: `1.24` + warm temp, suppressed blue curve
- Glow: `bloom 1.8`, `chromaticAberration 0.45`

## Iteration log

- v1: leakChance 1.0 made leaks a sticker → 0.6 keeps them an event.
- v2: leak palette constrained to orange/red-magenta/amber; blue leaks read
  as digital glitch, not seeping daylight.
