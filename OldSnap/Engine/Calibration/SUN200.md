# SUN 200 — '90s consumer 200-speed color neg · Calibration notes

**Reference material studied:** lab scans of 90s consumer 200-speed color
negative (golden-hour family shots, vacation scans, film-lab sample rolls).

## Observed characteristics

- **Golden warmth everywhere.** The stock's balance plus 90s minilab printing
  leans amber; whites are cream, never clinical.
- **Skin is the whole product.** Reds/oranges lifted through the skin range;
  blemishes melt; everyone looks slightly sun-kissed.
- **Gentle contrast.** Soft toe, very long shoulder — backlit shots hold both
  sky and face. Blacks slightly lifted and warm.
- **Fine, soft grain.** Present at 100%, invisible at print size — texture,
  not noise. Mostly luma.
- **Blue suppressed.** Skies go soft powder blue, not cobalt.

## Parameter mapping (→ `FilmStockLibrary.sun200`)

- Warmth: `temperature 0.38`, golden highlight tint (0.035, 0.025, 0)
- Skin lift: `curveR` raised across mids vs `curveG`/`curveB`
- Gentle contrast: shallow S-curves, `blackLift 0.035`, `highlightRolloff 0.8`
- Texture grain: `intensity 0.10, size 1.4, chromaMix 0.12`
- Muted sky: `curveB` shoulder ends at 0.95

## Iteration log

- v1: vibrance 0.25 made lawns radioactive → 0.14, saturation near-neutral.
- v2: warm shadow tint reduced 40%; reference shadows are warm but not sepia.
